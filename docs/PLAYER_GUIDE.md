# MIXMASH — Player Guide

*A DJ-powered platform fighter. Pick a headliner, pick a stage, knock your opponent off the platform.*

Play at **[mixmash.games/play/](https://mixmash.games/play/)** — no install, runs in the browser, 1 or 2 players on one keyboard (or gamepads).

## The basics

MIXMASH plays like a Smash-style platform fighter: there's no health bar. Landing hits raises your opponent's **damage %** — the higher it climbs, the further they fly when hit. Knock them off the stage and off the screen to score a KO.

- **Stock Match** — 3 stocks each, last DJ standing wins.
- **Time Match** — 3 minutes, most KOs wins.
- **Training** — practice mode with a hitbox display and instant respawn.
- **Platform Rush** — a solo route against the clock (see below).

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `A` / `D` | `←` / `→` |
| Jump | `W` | `↑` |
| Crouch / fast-fall | `S` | `↓` |
| Attack | `J` | `Numpad 1` |
| Attack (alt) | `Space` | — |
| Grab | `K` | `Numpad 2` |
| Shield | `L` | `Numpad 3` |

Gamepads work out of the box (left stick/d-pad to move, face button to attack, shoulder buttons to shield). Combine a direction with Attack for different moves — up-attack, down-attack, and forward-attack all hit differently, plus a separate aerial attack while airborne. Hold **Shield** to block; tap it to dodge. **Grab** into a throw when your opponent is shielding — grabs go through shields.

All of your control binds, volume, music toggle, hitbox-display, and stage-hazard settings are saved in your browser and reloaded automatically next time.

## Stages — 11 venues

Battlefield · Final Destination · Skybridge · Festival Stage · Tomorrowland · Ultra Miami · Burning Man · Coachella · EDC Las Vegas · Printworks · Electric Forest.

Each has a different platform layout — Skybridge, Festival Stage, Ultra Miami, EDC Las Vegas, Printworks, and Electric Forest have moving platforms — and its own parallax backdrop (lasers, fireworks, desert dust, crowds). **Stage Hazards** can be toggled off in Options if you just want to fight on a clean layout.

## Platform Rush

A solo mode for Player 1. You have **2 minutes** to collect **8 vinyl records** and **2 hidden Deep Cuts** spread across the stage. Records are worth 100 points each and Deep Cuts 350; Deep Cuts sit above the platforms and stay faint until you get close.

- Grab everything before time runs out for a **Platform Rush Clear** and a bonus of 10 points per second left on the clock. If the timer hits zero first, the run ends with **Time Up**.
- Your fastest clear on each stage is saved as your best time, and its route is saved as a **ghost** that runs alongside you the next time you play that layout.
- **Copy Daily Challenge** on the menu copies a link to today's Platform Rush layout on your selected stage, so friends can run the same route.

## Progress and challenges

Match stats, best Platform Rush times, ghosts, and challenge completions are saved locally in your browser (no account). Challenges: **First Match**, **First Win**, **Deep Cut Route** (complete every Platform Rush pickup), and **Encore Explorer** (play Printworks or Electric Forest).

## Tips

- Damage percent, not health — a fresh stock at 0% takes a real beating before it's in KO range; don't panic early.
- Shielding drains over time — don't hold it forever, or it'll break and leave you stunned.
- Off-stage recoveries matter: most DJs get a double jump (Avicii and Flume get a triple jump), so a hit near the edge isn't automatically a stock lost.
