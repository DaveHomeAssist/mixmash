# MIXMASH — Player Guide

*A DJ-powered platform fighter. Pick a headliner, pick a stage, knock your opponent off the platform.*

Play at **[mixmash.games/play/](https://mixmash.games/play/)** — no install, runs in the browser, 1 or 2 players on one keyboard (or gamepads).

## The basics

MIXMASH plays like a Smash-style platform fighter: there's no health bar. Landing hits raises your opponent's **damage %** — the higher it climbs, the further they fly when hit. Knock them off the stage and off the screen to score a KO.

- **Stock Match** — 3 stocks each, last DJ standing wins.
- **Time Match** — 3 minutes, most KOs wins.
- **Training** — practice mode with a hitbox display and instant respawn.

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `A` / `D` | `←` / `→` |
| Jump | `W` | `↑` |
| Crouch / fast-fall | `S` | `↓` |
| Attack | `J` | `Numpad 1` |
| Attack (alt) | `Space` | — |
| Grab | `K` | `Numpad 2` |
| Shield | `L` | `Numpad 3` |

Gamepads work out of the box (left stick/d-pad to move, face button to attack, shoulder buttons to shield). Combine a direction with Attack for different moves — up-attack, down-attack, and forward-attack all hit differently, plus a separate aerial attack while airborne. Hold **Shield** to block; tap it to dodge. **Grab** into a throw when your opponent is shielding — grabs go through shields.

All of your control binds, volume, music toggle, hitbox-display, and stage-hazard settings are saved in your browser and reloaded automatically next time.

## Stages — 9 venues

Battlefield · Final Destination · Skybridge · Festival Stage · Tomorrowland · Ultra Miami · Burning Man · Coachella · EDC Las Vegas.

Each has a different platform layout — some with moving platforms — and its own parallax backdrop (lasers, fireworks, desert dust, crowds). **Stage Hazards** can be toggled off in Options if you just want to fight on a clean layout.

## Tips

- Damage percent, not health — a fresh stock at 0% takes a real beating before it's in KO range; don't panic early.
- Shielding drains over time — don't hold it forever, or it'll break and leave you stunned.
- Off-stage recoveries matter: most DJs get a double jump (Avicii gets two), so a hit near the edge isn't automatically a stock lost.
