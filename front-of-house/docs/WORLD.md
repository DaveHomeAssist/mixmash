# Front of House World

The fictional acts, venues and places in the game, and a record of every name check ([CT-DEC-03](DECISIONS.md#ct-dec-03-artists-and-venues): the game's artists and venues are fictional). The numbers for each act live in `ARTISTS` in [`data.mjs`](../data.mjs); this page holds who they are.

## Acts on the Lot (tier 1)

| Act | Id | Genre | What they are like to book |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gravel Hymnal | `gravel-hymnal` | Folk | A small, cheap act with a loyal local crowd. Rarely the money-maker, but at $300 theirs is the roster's cheapest guarantee, and a guarantee always earns an act's trust. |
| Sodium Arcade | `sodium-arcade` | Indie rock | The first playable's act, named for the sodium streetlights over a parking lot. Plays for a guarantee or the door. |
| Juniper Switchboard | `juniper-switchboard` | Funk and soul | The Lot's biggest draw and the likeliest to sell it out. An established act that only plays for a guarantee. |

## Venues

| Venue | Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oak St. Lot | 1, the Lot | A rented parking lot, 24 by 16 tiles, permitted for 150 people. |
| Fathom Hall | 2, the Club | An indoor room, 20 by 14 tiles, permitted for 360, with a house PA and four pillars. |
| Loam Shell | 3, the Amphitheater | An outdoor shell, 28 by 18 tiles, permitted for 700, with 400 seats and holds of up to three nights. |
| Split Acre | 4, the Festival Grounds | A field, 40 by 24 tiles, permitted for 6,000, with a second stage, a sponsor and a broadcast. |
