The lot

The lot is 24 by 16 tiles. Q turns the view in quarter steps. Plus and minus zoom (or Ctrl and the mouse wheel), 0 shows the whole lot, and Shift with the arrow keys or a middle-button drag moves a zoomed view. The props stay painted for the original camera. In Build, click a tile or focus the board and use the arrow keys, Enter to place, R to rotate, B to bulldoze and Delete to remove.
